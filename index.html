Why Play on FunMax Games?

Every game on FunMax is built as a self-contained HTML5 app, so there is nothing to download and nothing to install. Open a game and it starts in under a second, whether you are on an Android phone, an iPhone, a tablet or a desktop browser. All six titles are free, and none of them require an account.

Prefer something slow and thoughtful? Start with our puzzle games — 2048 Number Merge rewards planning several moves ahead, and Memory Match trains short-term recall. Want something faster? The arcade section has Snake Classic, Breakout Brick Smash and Whack-a-Mole, all of which are about reflexes rather than strategy. Playing with someone else? Tic-Tac-Toe has both a two-player mode and an unbeatable AI opponent.

What Makes These Games Different

No interstitial ads between you and the game. No forced portrait orientation — every game adapts to landscape when your screen is wider than it is tall. Keyboard controls work on desktop, touch and swipe work on mobile, and your high scores and favourites are stored locally on your own device rather than on a server.
